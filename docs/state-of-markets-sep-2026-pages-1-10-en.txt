5
Tech Is The
Everything Cycle
© 2026 Andreessen Horowitz. All rights reserved worldwide. 5
Tech has gone from a “cottage industry” to the 
prime mover in the global economy. At this point, 
every company is, in some sense, a tech 
company–you’d be hard-pressed to find a truly
pen n’ paper business. 
More than any other sector, tech is driving 
investment, R&D cycles, earnings growth and 
margin expansion. The largest, most profitable,
and fastest-growing companies the world has
ever known, are tech companies. The AI 
infrastructure buildout alone is responsible for all
or nearly all of net-new construction spending,
blue collar job-growth, durable goods imports,
net-new IG-issuance, etc. AI infra is arguably the 
only pro-cyclical impulse firing in the economy 
right now.
The point is that tech has left the cottage. Tech is 
the Everything Cycle now, and it’s not looking back.
